
# Discussion

Mexican MPAs do not protect the core area within the EEZ used by Laysan albatrosses nesting in Mexico.

Only XXX percent of the core area falls inside MPAs.

In Mexico, MPAs are associated with islands, while the albatrosses’ core areas are associated with oceanographic conditions and food availability.

We question whether the Mexican MPAs protect the core areas used by the Laysan albatross breeding on Mexican islands.

The results of this study confirm what has been reported elsewhere  by @critchley2018marine and now confirmed also for  the Mexican Pacific.
There is low coverage of pelagic species by MPAs.

This is the first 12-year core-area analysis for Mexico’s albatross colonies.

The main limitation is that the data cover only the breeding season.
Therefore, the conclusions apply only to that period, and we cannot speak of MPA protection during the rest of the year.
However, given that it is during the breeding season that the population could be most vulnerable, this period is the most relevant for protection.

In the context of "30x30" commitments, our findings support a recommendation to expand protected areas to encompass the core areas identified in this study.
